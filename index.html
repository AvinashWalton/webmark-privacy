SECTION 01

Introduction

WebMark is a browser extension designed to help users highlight important text on webpages, create notes, and organize saved highlights through an extension dashboard.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use WebMark, how that information is handled, and what choices are available to you.

SECTION 02

Information processed

Depending on the features you use, WebMark may process:

  • Text that you select and choose to highlight.
  • Information needed to locate and restore saved highlights.
  • Notes and other content that you create in the extension.
  • Settings and records needed to operate the dashboard.
  • Data that you choose to import or export.

WebMark may access content on supported webpages to perform highlighting and restoration. It should not access more information than is reasonably necessary for its functionality.

SECTION 03

Local storage

WebMark is designed to store saved highlights, notes, and related extension data using browser storage on your device.

If the released version operates entirely locally, this information is not transmitted to a WebMark backend. Browser data can be affected by clearing extension storage, removing the extension, browser profile changes, or device failure.

Keep an exported backup of important information if the extension's export feature supports the data you need.

SECTION 04

Data sharing and sale

WebMark's intended local-first design does not require a WebMark account or a dedicated backend to store highlights and notes.

If the implementation does not transmit this information to external services, WebMark does not share or sell that locally stored information through such services.

Any analytics, remote services, third-party libraries that transmit data, or future server functionality must be disclosed here before being introduced in a published version.

SECTION 05

Webpage content

Highlighting requires interaction with webpage content. The extension may process selected text and surrounding page structure needed to create or restore a highlight.

WebMark is not intended to collect passwords, payment details, or unrelated private information. Avoid highlighting sensitive information, particularly on shared devices.

SECTION 06

Browser permissions

WebMark requests permissions needed for its implemented features. Depending on the published manifest, these may include:

  • storage for saving extension data.
  • activeTab for supported interactions with the active tab, if used.
  • downloads for browser download operations, if used.
  • Website access for content scripts that provide highlighting on supported pages.

The actual permission list is determined by the installed version. Users can review WebMark's permissions in their browser settings. Removing permissions that the extension requires may affect its features.

SECTION 07

Data retention and deletion

In a local-only implementation, saved information remains in browser storage until it is removed by the user, overwritten, or deleted through browser or extension actions.

You may be able to delete individual highlights or notes through the extension. Clearing the relevant extension storage or removing the extension may also remove saved information.

The exact behavior depends on the installed version and browser. Exported files may remain on your device until you delete them.

SECTION 08

Export and import

If export and import features are available, they allow you to save supported extension data to a file and restore it later.

Exported files may contain webpage text, highlights, or notes. Store these files securely and only import files from sources you trust.

SECTION 09

Your choices

  • Choose what text to highlight and what notes to create.
  • Remove saved items using available extension controls.
  • Review or revoke permissions through browser settings.
  • Remove WebMark if you no longer wish to use it.
  • Manage or delete exported files from your device.
SECTION 10

Children's privacy

WebMark is a general-purpose reading and productivity tool and is not specifically designed to collect children's personal data. If the product's audience or data practices change, this section should be updated to reflect the applicable requirements.

SECTION 11

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when WebMark's features, permissions, or data practices change. The revised policy will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

Please review this page periodically to stay informed about the current privacy practices.

SECTION 12

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or WebMark's privacy practices, contact the extension maintainer.

SECTION 01

Introduction

WebMark v1.1.0 is a browser extension designed to help users annotate supported webpages with a floating toolbar, apply highlight colors or text styles, create notes, organize saved items, and use reading and research tools through the extension dashboard.

This Privacy Policy describes the information the extension may process, how it is handled, and the choices available to users.

SECTION 02

Information processed

Depending on the features you use, WebMark v1.1.0 may process:

  • Text that you select and choose to highlight, underline, or strike through.
  • The selected highlight style, preset or custom color, and related annotation metadata.
  • Webpage titles, URLs, and domains associated with saved highlights and notes.
  • Tags, folder assignments, favorites, notes, and other organization details you create.
  • Information used to locate, detect duplicates, search, or restore saved highlights.
  • Reading-tool information such as word counts, estimated reading time, reading progress, and recent research items, where those features are used.
  • Extension preferences, including toolbar and restoration settings, and storage-management information.
  • Information included in JSON or Markdown exports and files you choose to import or restore.

The extension may access supported webpage content to perform highlighting and restoration. Avoid selecting sensitive information unless you are comfortable saving it.

SECTION 03

Local storage

WebMark v1.1.0 is designed to store highlights, notes, tags, folders, favorites, settings, and related feature data using browser storage on your device.

If the published implementation operates entirely locally, this information is not sent to a WebMark backend. Local information may be affected by clearing extension storage, removing the extension, changing browser profiles, or losing access to your device.

If the extension provides an export feature, consider keeping a backup of important information in a secure location.

SECTION 04

Data sharing and sale

WebMark does not require a WebMark user account to provide its intended local-first experience.

If the released version does not transmit saved information to external services, that information remains within the local storage environment instead of being sent to a WebMark server.

Any analytics, remote services, advertising technology, or third-party functionality that collects or transmits user information must be assessed and disclosed accurately before it is included in a published release.

SECTION 05

Webpage content

The floating toolbar and annotation tools interact with supported webpage content. WebMark may process selected text and relevant surrounding page structure to create, style, locate, or restore highlights. Reading tools may examine page text to calculate word count, estimated reading time, or reading progress when you choose to use them.

WebMark is not intended to collect passwords, payment details, or unrelated private information. Do not use highlights or notes to store information you would not want saved in your browser profile.

SECTION 06

Browser permissions

WebMark requests browser permissions to support its implemented features. Depending on the published manifest, these may include:

  • storage — saving extension data.
  • activeTab — temporary access to the active tab for supported interactions.
  • downloads — initiating downloads if the implementation uses this permission.
  • Website access — allowing content scripts to provide highlighting on supported webpages.

The actual permissions depend on the installed version. Users can review WebMark's permissions in their browser's extension settings. Restricting permissions may affect features.

SECTION 07

Data retention and deletion

In a local-only implementation, saved information remains in browser storage until it is deleted, overwritten, or removed through browser or extension actions.

You may be able to delete individual highlights or notes using the available extension controls. Clearing the relevant extension storage or uninstalling the extension may also remove saved information.

Exported files are separate from extension storage and may remain on your device until you delete them.

SECTION 08

Export and import

WebMark v1.1.0 provides data portability features, including JSON export/import and Markdown export where available in the installed build. Backup and restore can transfer compatible saved items and related metadata between supported installations.

Exported files may contain webpage addresses and titles, selected text, highlight styles and colors, notes, tags, folder names, favorites, and other included metadata. Markdown exports may present saved research in a readable document format. Keep exported files secure and import only files from sources you trust.

SECTION 09

Your choices

  • Choose which text to annotate and which notes to create.
  • Organize saved items using available tags, folders, and favorites.
  • Use or dismiss reading and research tools as desired.
  • Delete saved items or clear stored data using the available extension controls.
  • Review and manage extension permissions in your browser.
  • Export supported data as JSON or Markdown and restore compatible backups where available.
  • Remove WebMark if you no longer wish to use it.
  • Manage exported files stored on your device.
SECTION 10

Children's privacy

WebMark is a general-purpose reading and productivity extension. It is not intended to collect children's personal information through a dedicated online account or backend. Its actual data practices and applicable requirements should be reviewed before publication.

SECTION 11

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when WebMark's functionality, permissions, or data practices change. The latest revision date will be shown at the top of this page.

Users should review this page periodically to understand the privacy practices of the current release.

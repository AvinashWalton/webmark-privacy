# WebMark Privacy Policy **Last updated:** October 14, 2026 ## Overview WebMark is a browser extension designed to help users highlight text on webpages, save notes, and manage their highlights. WebMark is designed to operate locally in the user's browser without requiring an account or a dedicated backend server. ## Information Processed When you use WebMark, the extension may process the following information to provide its features: - Selected webpage text and information needed to identify and restore a highlight. - Highlights and notes created by the user. - Extension settings and saved data needed for the dashboard and supported import/export features. The extension may access webpage content on supported pages to perform the highlighting functionality requested by the user. ## Local Storage WebMark is designed to store its saved highlights, notes, and related information locally in the browser, using the browser's available storage mechanisms. **Do not publish this policy until you have verified that the actual extension code does not transmit this information to any remote server or third party.** Update this section if the implementation behaves differently. ## Data Sharing and Sale WebMark does not intentionally send saved highlights or notes to a WebMark-operated backend as part of its intended local-only design. WebMark does not sell users' saved highlights or notes. This statement does not cover browser-platform services or third-party services that may operate independently; those should be described if applicable. ## User Control and Data Deletion Users can manage saved highlights and notes through the extension's available features. Users can remove saved data through the extension or by clearing the relevant browser extension storage, where supported. Exported files are saved wherever the user chooses and are subject to the user's own file-management practices. ## Permissions WebMark requests browser permissions needed for its features. These permissions may allow it to access supported webpages, store extension data, and perform other declared actions. Users can review or revoke extension permissions through Microsoft Edge settings, although revoking required permissions may affect functionality. ## Children's Privacy WebMark is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. ## Changes to This Policy This policy may be updated when WebMark's functionality or data practices change.
